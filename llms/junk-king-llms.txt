# Junk King
> Junk King is a professional junk removal and hauling service brand offering full-service junk removal, dumpster rental, and eco-friendly disposal through locally owned and operated locations across the United States and Canada.

Junk King helps homeowners and businesses with residential and commercial junk removal, full-service hauling, self-service dumpster rental, dumpster bag pickup, appliance and furniture removal, and property cleanouts, with a focus on recycling and donation.
